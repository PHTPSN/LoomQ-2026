Quantum 101 · 新手入门

Quantum ideas,
translated into software.

You do not need a physics degree. For LoomQ, a quantum circuit is a short program that transforms a state, then samples an answer.

01 / DATA

Qubit

A quantum bit. Unlike a classical bit, its state can contain weighted |0⟩ and |1⟩ components before measurement.

02 / STATE

Superposition

A combination of basis states. Squaring each amplitude’s magnitude gives the probability of observing that state.

03 / CODE

Gate & circuit

A gate is one reversible state transformation. A circuit is the ordered instruction list—much like a tiny assembly program.

04 / OUTPUT

Measurement

Converts quantum information into classical bits. One measurement produces one bit string such as 00 or 11.

05 / SAMPLE

Shots

The number of times a circuit is prepared and measured. More shots make the observed histogram steadier.

06 / LINK

Entanglement

A joint state whose outcomes are correlated. A Bell pair behaves like two fair coins that always land on the same side.

A two-gate story

How a Bell pair is made

Start with two zeroes. The h gate creates two possible paths; cx links them. Measurement then returns mostly 00 or 11.

  1. Start|00⟩Known input
  2. Gateh q[0]Two paths
  3. Linkcx q[0],q[1]Entangle
  4. Measure00 ≈ 11Correlated counts

The official set · 官方门集

Twelve gates.
That is the whole vocabulary.

These are the only gates used by the competition circuits, including hidden cases. θ means a rotation angle in radians.

Single-qubit Phase & rotation Controlled & multi-qubit
01h1 qubit

Hadamard

Creates or recombines equal quantum paths—the usual doorway into superposition.

h q[0];
02x1 qubit

Bit flip

Swaps |0⟩ and |1⟩. It is the quantum counterpart of classical NOT.

x q[0];
03s1 qubit

Quarter phase

Adds a 90° phase to the |1⟩ component. Interference makes that hidden phase observable.

s q[0];
04sdg1 qubit

Inverse S

Removes the S phase, applying −90° instead. “dg” means dagger, or inverse.

sdg q[0];
05t1 qubit

Eighth phase

Adds a finer 45° phase. T gates are important building blocks for general algorithms.

t q[0];
06tdg1 qubit

Inverse T

Applies the opposite −45° phase, undoing a T gate when placed directly after it.

tdg q[0];
07rz(θ)1 qubit

Z rotation

Rotates phase by any angle θ. It changes interference, although direct Z-basis probabilities may stay unchanged.

rz(pi/2) q[0];
08ry(θ)1 qubit

Y rotation

Rotates amplitudes by any angle θ, directly changing the chance of measuring zero or one.

ry(pi/2) q[0];
09cx2 qubits

Controlled X

Flips the target only when the control is one. With H, it is the standard recipe for entanglement.

cx q[0],q[1];
10cu1(θ)2 qubits

Controlled phase

Adds phase θ only when both qubits are one. It is common in Fourier-style circuits.

cu1(pi/2) q[0],q[1];
11swap2 qubits

Exchange

Trades the complete states of two qubits without measuring either one.

swap q[0],q[1];
12ccx3 qubits

Toffoli

Flips the target only when both controls are one—a reversible version of classical AND logic.

ccx q[0],q[1],q[2];
